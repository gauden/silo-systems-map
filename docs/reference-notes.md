# Reference interaction notes

Inspected on 6 September 2026: https://slides.gaudengalea.com/reclaiming/cdoh.html

The user specifically requested its link treatment: curved directed links, visible strokes, blue positive links and red negative links. Signs remain visible and arrowheads follow link colour. The Silo implementation adapts these colours for a dark canvas; its dashed links indicate the uncertainty explicitly present in MAP.dot.

The user also requested selection to focus a node and its first-degree peers, re-layout and fit, and dragging that makes surrounding nodes react. The Silo viewer applies these interactions to its own supplied data. It does not copy the reference's causal model, cases, evidence claims or descriptions.

The Model/Loops/Pathways navigation and a textual detail panel retain the reference's exploration pattern. Cases and authoring are excluded from the approved Silo MVP.

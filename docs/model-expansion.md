# Connections and contradictions

The expanded DOT is a causal interpretation of the supplied Silo model, not a catalogue of established plot errors. All original 36 variables and 47 relationships remain intact. Eleven variables and 34 explicitly inferred relationships connect surveillance, informal knowledge, enforcement, repair and collective coordination. The result contains 356 simple directed cycles, including 172 reinforcing and 184 balancing cycles; 198 contain uncertain relationships.

Eight contradictions compare causal branches: adaptation under prohibition; knowledge without a past; paper without a public sphere; control needing suppressed knowledge; interdependence across distance; scarcity versus forbidden reuse; safe machinery versus unsafe truths; and maintenance of surveillance. Each definition lives in MAP.dot with explanations, edge references and a caveat. The viewer derives the subgraph from those references.

“Who maintains the watchers?” connects manufacturing, replacement electronics, covert maintenance, technology restrictions and ageing. Generic manufacturing does not establish semiconductor fabrication. Stockpiles, privileged workshops or other support could sustain hidden cameras and recording devices; the model exposes that dependency without asserting these supports cannot exist.

Paper availability alone does not imply printing, secure distribution or durable independent publication. The user-supplied paper premise is modelled alongside censorship and enforcement, with uncertain links where capacity does not guarantee outcomes.
